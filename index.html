☀️ ABOUT OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

Our Solar System formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago. It consists of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets.

The Sun is at the center of our Solar System. Its gravity keeps the planets in orbit around it.
